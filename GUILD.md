## Charter (draft)

Axios is two raid teams plus a community house. Same house, two rosters. Not a hardcore top-end guild. Alliance, Classic+. First wave is people we already raided with, invited on Discord.

Show up ready on your team’s nights. Bring consumables and a working mic when we raid. Don’t be a jerk in Discord or in game. Alts are fine. A raid spot is earned on that team, not inherited from another.

Until Classic+ has realms, Discord is the guild.

### House rules that do not change with patch notes

- Be an adult. Don’t be a dick.
- No slurs, no bigotry, no politics in Discord or guild chat.
- Don’t buy gold. Don’t sell loot. Don’t sell ranks.
- Raid or loot problem → talk to the GM. Don’t turn it into a public fight.
- One main raid team per person. Alts can help the other team if that lead asks.
- Repairs and consumes follow who actually shows up.
- Switching teams means both raid leads and the GM are in the conversation.
- Team 1 does not get first pick of Team 2’s loot. Separate lockouts, separate loot pools.
- Community is the house. It is not a third raid and does not get a raid lockout.

### Pipeline vs independent — TBD (Brick)

This is the Thursday loot-draft fork. Pick one and write it here. Neither option names a third raid. Neither option assigns Team 1 or Team 2 as casual or semi-hardcore.

**Pipeline:** One team feeds the other. Same house, different intensity. Openings on the receiving team go to the other team first. A failed trial on the receiving team can land on the other team instead of out. Loot still per team / per lockout. Risk: one team feels like a waiting room.

**Independent:** Two real teams. Neither is a feeder or the other’s alt night. One main team per person. Alts help the other only if that lead asks. Loot never crosses. Risk: you have to staff two rosters for real.

Either way: separate lockouts, separate loot pools, oversized roster so we do not run a 100% attendance church. Community stays the house, not a raid.
