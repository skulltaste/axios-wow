The house

Community first

Axios is a large community with raid teams. Same house. Not a hardcore top-end guild. We are standing up for Classic+, Alliance. Realm stays TBD.

The three bars on the A are the house: community, casual, and semi-hardcore. Casual and semi-hardcore are raid intensities, not named teams. Community is the house. It is not a third raid.

  • Community

    The house. Friends, alts, plus-ones, people between rosters. Not a raid lockout.

  • Casual

    A raid intensity. Show up, clear, stay human. Not a leftover bench.

  • Semi-hardcore

    A raid intensity. Ready on the nights, consumes and a mic. Not a cutting-edge church.

No hardcore top end. Team names stay unset. Do not ask the site for a realm — there isn’t one yet.

Charter

House rules

Axios is a large community with raid teams. First wave is people we already raided with. Show up ready on your team’s nights. Bring consumables and a working mic when we raid. Don’t be a jerk in Discord or in game. Alts are fine. A raid spot is earned on that team, not inherited from another. Until Classic+ has realms, Discord is the guild.

The basics

  • Be an adult. Don’t be a dick.
  • No slurs, no bigotry, no politics in Discord or guild chat.
  • Don’t buy gold. Don’t sell loot. Don’t sell ranks.
  • Raid or loot problem → talk to the GM. Don’t turn it into a public fight.

Living together

  • One main raid team per person. Alts can help another team if that lead asks.
  • Repairs and consumes follow who actually shows up.
  • Switching teams means both raid leads and the GM are in the conversation.
  • Separate lockouts, separate loot pools. One team does not get first pick of another team’s drops.
  • Community is the house. It is not a third raid and does not get a raid lockout.
