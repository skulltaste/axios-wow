Who we are

Axios is a Classic+ Alliance community. Raid teams live under one roof — casual and semi-hardcore. No hardcore top end. Realm TBD until Classic+ has one.

  • Community

    The house. Friends, alts, and people between rosters.

  • Casual

    Raid at a human pace. Show up, clear, stay kind.

  • Semi-hardcore

    Ready on raid nights. Consumes and a mic.

The mark’s three bars are those three pieces of the same house.

House rules

First wave is people we already raided with. Until realms exist, Discord is the guild.

The basics

  • Be an adult. Don’t be a dick.
  • No slurs, no bigotry, no politics in Discord or guild chat.
  • Don’t buy gold. Don’t sell loot. Don’t sell ranks.
  • Raid or loot problem → talk to the GM. Don’t turn it into a public fight.

Living together

  • One main raid team per person. Alts can help another team if that lead asks.
  • A raid spot is earned on that team, not inherited from another.
  • Repairs and consumes follow who actually shows up.
  • Switching teams means both raid leads and the GM are in the conversation.
  • Separate lockouts, separate loot pools. One team does not get first pick of another team’s drops.
