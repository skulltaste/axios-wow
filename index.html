House rules

The full charter lives in Discord. This is the part that does not change when the patch notes do.

The basics

  • Be an adult. Don’t be a dick.
  • No slurs, no bigotry, no politics in Discord or guild chat.
  • Don’t buy gold. Don’t sell loot. Don’t sell ranks.
  • Raid or loot problem → talk to the GM. Don’t turn it into a public fight.

Living together

  • One main raid team. Alts can help another team if that lead asks.
  • Repairs and consumes follow who actually shows up.
  • Apex does not get first pick of Hearth’s loot.
  • Switching teams means both raid leads and the GM are in the conversation.

Apply

Tanks and raid healers are always needed. Everything else is open until the announce, then we get picky.

  1. Read this page and join Discord.
  2. Grab Apex, Vanguard, or Hearth in #roles.
  3. Post in #recruitment-info or email us with Battle.net, Discord, timezone, team, class/role, logs or “this will be my first 60,” and hours a week you actually have.

We’ll reply. You don’t need a novel.
